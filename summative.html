The States of Matter Final Assignment

Project Overview:
In this project, you will explore the three states of matter—solid, liquid, and gas—through hands-on experiments and interactive activities. You will learn about how matter changes between these states through heating, cooling, and pressure changes, and you will present your findings in a creative way.

Objectives

  • Understand the properties of solids, liquids, and gases.
  • Observe how matter changes between the three states.
  • Demonstrate your understanding through experiments, quizzes, and presentations.
  • Reflect on how the states of matter are observed in everyday life.

Experiment 1: Freezing Point

Materials

Water, container, freezer

Procedure

Freeze water and observe the change from liquid to solid.

Experiment 2: Boiling Point

Materials

Water, stove, thermometer

Procedure

Heat water and observe the transformation to steam (gas).
